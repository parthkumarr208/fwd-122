About the lab

Research for better-performing systems

The Performance Modeling and Simulation Lab (PMSL) at South Asian University studies the performance of computer systems, especially modern communication networks and emerging technologies.

This website introduces the lab, its research focus, members, and academic work. It is designed to make information easy to explore on desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

What we study

Research areas

Our work includes modeling, simulation, and performance analysis across areas such as:

Communication networks

IoT traffic modeling and the performance of modern network systems.

5G networks

Modeling and analyzing traffic and performance in 5G environments.

Blockchain

Performance analysis of blockchain systems and related technologies.

Emerging technologies

Research topics include Web 3, federated machine learning, and software cost estimation.

Probabilistic analysis

Studying system behavior, including probabilistic attack detection.
